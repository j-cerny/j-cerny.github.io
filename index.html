jakub černý.

Jakub is an associate research scientist in the IEOR department at Columbia and director of research in Christian Kroer’s group. his research focuses on operations research and computation. with the Office of Naval Research, he creates decision-support logistics tools for US Navy. he also works with Natural State on biodiversity monitoring, and Mar Futura on deterring illegal fishing. he received his phd from NTU.
